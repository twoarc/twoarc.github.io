Privacy Policy

This privacy policy applies to TwoArc Autopilot (the "Tool"), an internal desktop tool operated by Kaptan Kaan Yıldırım, TwoArc (the "Operator"). The Tool is used only with TikTok accounts owned or managed by the Operator to publish promotional videos for the Operator's own mobile apps.

Information the Tool accesses

When a TikTok account is connected through TikTok Login Kit, the Tool receives only the data allowed by the permissions the account owner approves:

  • Basic profile information (open ID, display name, avatar) to show which account is connected.
  • Access and refresh tokens issued by TikTok, used to call the TikTok API on behalf of the connected account.
  • Statistics of the account's own videos (video ID, caption, creation time, view, like, comment and share counts) to measure which promotional videos perform best.

The Tool does not access other users' data, private messages, followers lists, or any data about viewers.

How information is used and stored

  • Data is used only to upload the Operator's videos as drafts and to measure their performance.
  • Tokens and statistics are stored locally on the Operator's own computer. They are not uploaded to any server operated by TwoArc.
  • Data is never sold, rented, or shared with third parties, and is not used for advertising profiles.
  • The Tool may send the Operator a notification (for example via the Operator's own Telegram bot) containing the generated video and its caption. No TikTok user data is included.

Retention and deletion

Video statistics are kept for as long as they are useful for performance analysis. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings and privacy → Security and permissions → Apps and services permissions). After revocation the Tool can no longer access the account, and stored tokens become invalid. To request deletion of any stored data, contact the Operator at the address below.

Third-party services

The Tool uses the TikTok for Developers APIs. TikTok's own Privacy Policy applies to data processed by TikTok.

Children

The Tool is not directed at children and is only used by the Operator.
