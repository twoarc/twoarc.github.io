Privacy Policy

Ranking Filter: Fun Challenge
Last updated: July 1, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how 2Arc ("2Arc," "we," "us," or "our") handles information in the Ranking Filter: Fun Challenge mobile application (the "Application").

The Application is provided as a free entertainment application. It allows users to create ranking-style videos using camera features, visual effects, and filter content.

Summary: We do not require accounts and do not ask you to provide your name, email address, phone number, or similar direct profile information. However, the Application uses Google AdMob and Firebase Analytics, which may process certain device, identifier, advertising, diagnostic, and app-usage information as described below.

1. Information We Process

1.1 Information you do not provide to us

The Application does not require user registration. We do not ask you to create an account or directly provide your name, email address, phone number, postal address, password, or payment information.

1.2 Camera, microphone, and video content

The Application may request access to your device camera and microphone to enable recording features. It may also request access to photos, videos, or media storage when you choose to save an output video to your device gallery.

Videos created in the Application, including the related visual effects processing, are processed locally on your device. We do not upload your raw recordings, processed videos, camera feed, or microphone recordings to 2Arc servers.

If you choose to save a completed video, it is saved to your device gallery under the controls and permissions of your device operating system. We do not receive a copy of that saved video.

1.3 On-device face-related processing

The Application may use on-device face detection or similar local visual processing to support interactive camera effects. This processing is not used to identify or authenticate you. We do not upload, store, sell, or share facial templates, biometric identifiers, or facial recognition data.

1.4 Device, usage, and technical information

Through Firebase Analytics, Google Mobile Ads SDK, and related Google services, certain information may be collected or processed automatically. Depending on your device, operating system, app version, region, consent choices, and the advertising or analytics features in use, this may include:

  • device type, operating system, language, and app version;
  • device and app identifiers, including advertising identifiers where available;
  • Internet Protocol (IP) address and network-related information;
  • approximate location inferred from IP address or device/network signals;
  • app interactions and usage information, such as app opens, sessions, filter selections, completed filters, share-button taps, and navigation-related events;
  • advertising information, including ad requests, impressions, ad performance, and interactions with advertisements;
  • diagnostics, crash-related information, and information used to improve app stability and performance.

The Application does not collect precise GPS location information. Third-party services may nevertheless infer an approximate location from your IP address or network information.

We and our service providers may use information to:

  • operate, maintain, and improve the Application;
  • provide recording, processing, saving, and sharing features;
  • understand general application usage and feature performance;
  • measure events such as filter openings, completed videos, and sharing interactions;

  • detect abuse, fraud, technical problems, and security incidents;

Depending on your region and privacy choices, advertisements may be personalized, non-personalized, limited, or otherwise configured in accordance with Google’s available advertising products and applicable requirements.

We use analytics data in aggregated or event-based form to understand overall product usage. We do not use Firebase Analytics to create user accounts in the Application or ask you to provide direct profile information.

In delivering and securing these assets, Cloudflare may process network-related information, such as IP address and request logs, in accordance with its own privacy practices.
