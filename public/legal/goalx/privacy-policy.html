GoalX Privacy Policy

Effective date: 12 September 2026

This privacy policy explains how the GoalX: Football Prediction application ("Application") handles personal data. The Application is developed and operated by 2Arc ("we", "us"), which is the data controller for the processing described here. You can reach us at any time at twoarc@proton.me.

The Application is free to use, offers optional paid subscriptions, and displays third-party advertising.

1. Accounts

When you open the Application for the first time, a guest account is created automatically so that you can use the app without registering. This account holds a randomly generated username and your in-app activity.

You may later link your guest account to a permanent account using an email address and password, or by signing in with Google, Facebook or Apple. Linking keeps your existing data and adds your email address to your profile. If you sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email address, we only ever receive Apple's private relay address.

3. Analytics

We use PostHog to understand how the Application is used. Analytics events are linked to your account: we send your user identifier, username, account type and, for registered accounts, your email address. This data is therefore not anonymous. PostHog processes it on servers in the European Union on our behalf.

4. Automated accounts and artificial intelligence

Some public clubs include automated chat accounts that post messages generated by an artificial-intelligence model. These accounts are labelled "Automated chat account" on their profile page, and the Application tells you about them before you post your first message.

To generate a reply that fits the conversation, the recent messages of that club's chat are sent to Google (Gemini API). Usernames are removed before sending and replaced by neutral labels such as "User A"; the message text itself is sent. Google processes this data as our service provider in order to return the generated reply.

If you would rather your messages were not processed this way, do not post in public clubs that contain automated accounts. No other feature of the Application uses artificial intelligence to process your data.

5. Push notifications

If you allow notifications, your device issues a notification token that we store with your profile and use to send goal alerts, match reminders, club chat alerts and occasional service messages. Delivery goes through the Expo push service and from there to Apple's Push Notification service on iOS, or to Firebase Cloud Messaging on Android. You can withdraw this permission at any time in your device settings, and the stored token is removed when you delete your account.

6. Advertising

The Application displays advertising supplied by Google AdMob. Subscribers to GoalX Premium do not see advertising.

Whether advertising is personalised depends on your consent:

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland we ask for your consent through Google's consent form when you first open the Application. You can change your answer at any time from Settings → Privacy Choices inside the Application.
  • On iOS we additionally ask, through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt, whether the Application may access your advertising identifier. If you decline, the identifier is not used and advertising is not personalised.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.

Where personalised advertising is enabled, your advertising identifier and related ad interaction data are shared with Google and its advertising partners. Under certain laws, including the California Consumer Privacy Act, this may qualify as "sharing" or a "sale" of personal information for targeted advertising. Declining tracking, or withdrawing consent through Privacy Choices, opts you out.

On Android, Google's Mobile Ads SDK also includes Google's advertising measurement component, which reports whether an advertisement was shown or interacted with. On iOS no such component is included.

If you see an advertisement that is inappropriate or not suitable for your age, you can report it from Settings → Report an Ad.

7. Subscriptions and purchases

GoalX Premium is billed by Apple or Google, not by us, and we never receive your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to verify and synchronise your subscription status; RevenueCat receives a pseudonymous user identifier together with the purchase and entitlement data reported by the store.

8. Service providers

We share data only with providers that process it on our behalf under contract, and only for the purposes described above:

  • Supabase — database, authentication and backend hosting (European Union)
  • PostHog — product analytics (European Union)
  • Expo — application delivery, updates and push notifications
  • Google AdMob — advertising
  • Google — Sign-In, the Gemini API (see section 4), Google Play services, and Firebase Cloud Messaging for push delivery on Android. The Application uses no other Firebase product and includes no Firebase software on iOS.
  • RevenueCat — subscription management
  • Apple — Sign in with Apple, App Store billing and push delivery
  • Meta / Facebook — Facebook Login only

Some of these providers are established outside the European Economic Area. Where data is transferred there, the transfer is covered by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or another lawful transfer mechanism.

We may also disclose data where the law requires it, where it is necessary to protect the rights or safety of our users or of others, or to investigate fraud or abuse.

9. Legal bases

Where the General Data Protection Regulation applies, we rely on: performance of a contract for running your account, predictions, leagues, clubs and subscriptions; consent for personalised advertising and push notifications; legitimate interests for analytics, security, abuse prevention and improving the Application; and legal obligation where disclosure is required. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time, without affecting processing that already took place.

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR, the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law (KVKK) and comparable laws you may request access to your data, correction of inaccurate data, deletion, restriction of processing, a portable copy, and you may object to processing based on legitimate interests. You also have the right to complain to your national supervisory authority.

To exercise any of these rights, write to twoarc@proton.me from the address registered to your account. We respond within 30 days.

11. Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its data from inside the Application at any time: Profile → Settings → Delete Account. This is immediate and cannot be undone. It removes your profile, predictions, club messages, blocks and notification token from our database, detaches you from subscription management, and requests erasure of your analytics record.

If you cannot access the Application, email twoarc@proton.me from your registered address and we will delete the account within 30 days of verifying your identity.

Deleting your GoalX account does not cancel an App Store or Google Play subscription. Cancel it separately in your store settings to avoid further charges.

12. Retention

We keep account data for as long as your account exists. Automatically collected usage data is kept for up to 24 months, after which it is aggregated or deleted. Reports and moderation records may be kept longer where that is necessary to protect other users. Some records may be retained where the law requires it.

13. Children

The Application is not directed at children under 13, or under the higher minimum age that applies in your country, and we do not knowingly collect their personal data. If we learn that we hold data belonging to a child below that age we delete it. Parents and guardians can contact us at twoarc@proton.me.

14. Security

Access to personal data is restricted, data is transmitted over encrypted connections, and database access is governed by row-level security rules so that users cannot read each other's private data. No system can be guaranteed completely secure, but we take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your information.

15. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The effective date at the top of this page always shows the current version, and significant changes are announced in the Application. Continuing to use the Application after a change means you accept the updated policy.

16. Contact

Questions about this policy or about how your data is handled: twoarc@proton.me
